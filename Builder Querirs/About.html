Our Mission

BuilderQueries was founded to solve a persistent problem in Ghana's construction and services industry — finding skilled, reliable contractors was hard. Too much of it happened through word-of-mouth, leaving most people taking chances with unverified workers.

We built a platform where every professional is vetted, every project is protected, and every payment is secure until the job is done right.
